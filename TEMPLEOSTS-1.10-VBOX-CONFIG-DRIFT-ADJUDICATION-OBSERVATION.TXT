host or guest integration behaviour.  They must not be normalized away merely
to make the adopted baseline pass.

are exact to the live VDI and its NTFS file ID is distinct.  It is therefore a
disk-exact recovery asset.  Its VBOX file is not an exact copy of the current
registered VM configuration.
