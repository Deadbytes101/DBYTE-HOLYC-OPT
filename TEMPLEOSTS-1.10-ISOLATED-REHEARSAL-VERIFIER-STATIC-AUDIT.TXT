ADVERSARIAL JUDGMENT

The verifier intentionally treats a byte change caused by a nominally read-only
VBoxSVC session as a verification failure.  The manifest inventory is rehashed
before and after isolated registry access.  A changed, added or removed file
prevents the powered-off environment from being sealed.

The verifier does not prove a guest boot or destructive restore drill.  A pass may
seal only the exact prepared powered-off rehearsal environment.
