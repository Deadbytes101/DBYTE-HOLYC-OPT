CORRECTED CARDINALITY CONTRACT

For every R2 replacement:

  old_count_before == expected_count
  new_count_before == 0
  new_count_after == expected_count

    exact complete occurrences of Old contained inside New

  expected_old_count_after

  old_count_after == expected_old_count_after

new block to intentionally retain one complete old line.
