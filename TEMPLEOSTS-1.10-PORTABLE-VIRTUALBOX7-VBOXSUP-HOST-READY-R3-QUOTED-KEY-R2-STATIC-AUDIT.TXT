ROOT CAUSE VERIFIED

The bound base verifier parser captures a raw machine-readable key as:

    $Key = $Matches[1]

It removes surrounding quotes from values, but not keys.
VirtualBox 7.0.12 emits storage keys quoted while ordinary keys such as VMState are unquoted.
Therefore the map stored literal quote characters around IDE-1-0 and the later unquoted required-key lookup failed.

CORRECTION 1

Exact source occurrence:

    $Key = $Matches[1]

is required exactly once and replaced exactly once with logic that:
- casts the captured key to string;
- checks length >= 2;
- checks first and last characters are [char]34;
- removes exactly those two surrounding quote characters;
- otherwise leaves the key unchanged.

No general key rewriting, case folding, whitespace normalization, delimiter change, or storage-specific hardcoding is introduced.
