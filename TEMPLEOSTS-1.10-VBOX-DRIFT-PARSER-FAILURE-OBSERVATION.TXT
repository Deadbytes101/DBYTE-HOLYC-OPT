ROOT CAUSE

VBoxManage machine-readable storage attachment keys can be emitted with quoted
keys, for example:

  "IDE-1-0"="D:\VirtualBox VMs\Temple1.10\Temple1.10.vdi"

The parser removed quotes and escapes from values but retained quotes around
keys.  The resulting hashtable contained the key "IDE-1-0" including quote
characters, while the assertion requested IDE-1-0 without quote characters.

The failure is a parser representation mismatch.  It is not evidence of a
missing VDI attachment.
