usage surfaces.  Therefore exit code zero is not a valid availability criterion for
this exact version.

The matrix also proves, across four independent forms, the exact supported switch
spellings required by the preparer:

  clipboard:
    --clipboard-mode

  drag-and-drop:
    --drag-and-drop

The broader substring marker --clipboard is also true because --clipboard-mode
contains it.  It is not independent evidence for selecting the shorter --clipboard
spelling.  The exact --clipboard-mode marker is the authoritative clipboard result.

The compact drag-and-drop spelling:

  --draganddrop

was false in every measured form and must not be selected.
