PIPELINE ADJUDICATION

Compiler/CmpExts.CPP contains extern declarations for the optimizer passes.  It
is not the compile-pipeline owner.

Compiler/ICLCC.CPP defines LCCCompile.  Exact displayed source proves that
LCCCompile owns the optimizer-to-sizing-to-emission sequence:

  OptPass012
  OptPass012
  OptPass3
  OptPass4
  OptPass5
  OptPass6
  jump-table label forwarding
  OptPass789 sizing until stable
  AOT or JIT allocation
  OptPass789 final emission

The strongest candidate boundary is after jump-table label forwarding and
before the first OptPass789 sizing call.  LCCHeaderPut(lx,7,FALSE) remains in
that interval and its pass-state contract must be preserved.

The complete adjudication is recorded in:

  TEMPLEOSTS-1.10-PIPELINE-ADJUDICATION.TXT
